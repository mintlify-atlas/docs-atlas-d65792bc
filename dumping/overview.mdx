Unleashed Recompiled does not include any game assets — you must supply the files yourself from a legally owned Xbox 360 copy of Sonic Unleashed. This section walks you through everything you need and the two available methods for getting those files onto your PC.

## What You Need

To install Unleashed Recompiled you will need the following items from your Xbox 360:

- **Sonic Unleashed for Xbox 360** — US or EU release only. Available as a retail disc or as a digital purchase from the [Xbox Store](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/sonic-unleashed/c098fgnmts8f).
- **Title Update** — required for the installer to accept your files.
- **DLC — Adventure Packs** — optional, but very strongly recommended (see note below).

<Warning>
  The Japanese (JP) release of Sonic Unleashed is **not supported**. Its file structure and content differ from the international releases in ways that make it incompatible with Unleashed Recompiled. The US and EU versions already include full Japanese language support, so there is no functional difference for Japanese-speaking players.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The Adventure Pack DLC is **highly recommended**. In addition to the extra stages, the DLC includes **high-quality lighting for the entire game** — not just the DLC content itself. Playing without it means the whole game will use lower-quality lighting.
</Note>

## Tools Required

You will need the following tools on your PC before you begin:

- [**Velocity** (Gualdimar's fork)](https://github.com/Gualdimar/Velocity/releases/download/xex%2Biso-branch/Velocity-XEXISO.rar) — used to read the Xbox 360 storage device and copy the game files.
- [**7-Zip**](https://7-zip.org/download.html) — needed to extract the Velocity `.rar` archive.

## Choose a Dumping Method

There are two ways to get the game files from your Xbox 360 to your PC. Pick the one that matches your setup:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Hard Drive" icon="hard-drive" href="/dumping/hard-drive">
    Connect your Xbox 360 hard drive directly to your PC using the Xbox 360 Hard Drive Transfer Cable (or a compatible SATA to USB adapter) and copy the files with Velocity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="USB Drive" icon="usb-drive" href="/dumping/usb">
    Configure a USB storage device as a system drive on your Xbox 360, install the game to it, then connect the USB drive to your PC and copy the files with Velocity.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
